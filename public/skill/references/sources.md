# Evidence and limits

This skill contains independently authored design procedures and original synthesis. Its supporting research included public design material and a bounded transcript review; no video-frame inspection or complete-channel review was performed. Runtime guidance is generalized rather than attributed to an individual. Do not present a rule as a quotation, endorsement, universal aesthetic law or measured user benefit.

## Standards

- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) grounds verification thresholds. Check criterion applicability and exceptions; this checklist is not certification.
- The [UK interaction-designer framework](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/interaction-designer/) and [whole-problem service standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-2-solve-a-whole-problem) inform outcome, evidence, risk and unresolved-decision distinctions.
- The [HEART publication](https://research.google/pubs/measuring-the-user-experience-on-a-large-scale-user-centered-metrics-for-web-applications/) was reviewed at abstract level only.

## Evidence discipline

Separate inspected artifacts from assumptions, proposals from executed results, and expert judgment from user research. Report incomplete evidence and blocked tests explicitly. Formatting validation or source-only exercises cannot establish visual quality, runtime behavior or causal effectiveness. The decision contract, craft recipes and evaluation rubric are working synthesis, not copied standards or conformance claims.

The package includes no copied picker implementation or substantial licensed source text. If adding licensed material later, preserve its required notices. Keep research history separate from operational instructions.
