## Kole Jain transcript evidence, checked 2026-11-16

The following source observations were relayed by the research worker. They are transcript-derived, not direct screen inspection by the skill author. Publication dates and precise timestamps were not verified in this package; any transcript timings may drift. Evidence modes are recorded per entry below. A complete transcript is not complete video or visual review.

- Dashboard: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ksx9C2-3yMo . Evidence: complete transcript, no frames. Observations: numeric comparison alignment; purposeful category chips; access to truncated values; chronological activity as a timeline/drawer; frequent actions visible, less-used actions disclosed; search within sharing selection; detailed copy/comment/drawer/tooltip states. Synthesis: select density treatments by actual task frequency and test full-value access, rather than minimizing everything.
- Dashboard: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B7k5rOgmOGY . Evidence: complete transcript, no frames. Observations: concise navigation with explicit current state; chart scale, date and summary; bulk actions after selection; bounded tasks in popovers/modals versus durable page context. Synthesis: choose container by complexity and context retention. Optimistic rollback, failure-path and focus-return checks are added safeguards from this skill, not attributed creator statements.
- Beginner design: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AH_ugxmLeUM . Evidence: complete transcript, no frames. Observations: visible skip/custom paths, consistent recurring spacing and controls/icon styles, immediate pressed/loading feedback and chart axes. Synthesis: make the next/alternative path and action response observable; normalize repeated structures before adding decoration.
- Product thinking: https://sozai.app/transcript/stop-making-pretty-uis-think-product-designer/ . Evidence: complete transcript, no frames. Observations: empty/loading/success/error states, entry/next/skip paths and reused semantics. Synthesis: audit the flow surrounding the attractive screen, not just its static composition.
- Color ratios: https://sozai.app/transcript/60-30-10-rule-ruining-ui-designs/ . Evidence: transcript excerpt, no frames. Observation: rejects rigid color percentages in favor of semantic background, stroke and text levels, with separately tuned dark mode and status/chart colors. Synthesis: use semantic tokens and test contrast/noncolor cues; never force a decorative ratio onto an operational interface.
- SaaS: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDcQJOPby1k . Evidence: transcript excerpt, no frames. Observations: duplicate KPIs in unrelated positions, consolidated controls, rare row actions in overflow, actual cost and billing interval emphasis. Synthesis: remove informational repetition only after preserving decision context, and keep financial units/intervals explicit.

- AI components: https://sozai.app/transcript/7-ui-components-design-unicorn-ai/ . Evidence: partial transcript through approximately 4:50, no frames. Relayed observations include attachment previews, context chips, searchable history, scoped editing, meaningful progress and skeletons. Treat these as excerpt-level observations, not a review of the entire video. Synthesis: make input context and operation scope inspectable; show progress honestly rather than inventing completion percentages.
