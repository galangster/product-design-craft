# Compare structural directions

State one decision question. Default to three materially different structures, or fewer when only two credible alternatives exist; honor an explicitly requested count. Hold content, task, data and design system constant. Vary layout, reading order or interaction model, not merely colors. Name each variant by its tradeoff and tell the user what to try.

Follow the accessible prototype workflow for the active agent/project. Retain this skill's safety and evidence checks: a dependency's shortcuts or cleanup steps do not authorize production edits, commits, issue changes or publication.

Prefer real app context in a safe development preview with mutations stubbed. Make switching obvious, shareable and keyboard-safe without intercepting editable fields; exclude demo controls from production. Inspect alternatives full-size, not only as miniatures.

For state-model uncertainty, a small standalone demo with readable state, free exploration and resettable scenarios may answer the question better than polished layouts. Controls work or are explicitly labeled nonfunctional. The completion test is whether the artifact answers the stated question. Choosing a direction is distinct from authorizing production integration.
